Demo App is a demo entry on this site. This page shows how a support page
for a small, offline TurkWave app of this kind is laid out.

## Getting help

Demo App runs entirely on your device and has no accounts or sign-in. Most
questions are answered on the Demo App page and in its FAQ.

## Contact

Email the address shown on the Demo App page. Replies are usually sent
within a few business days.

## Reporting a problem

When reporting a bug, include your device model, operating-system version and
the app version, along with the steps that reproduce the issue.

## Feature requests

Suggestions are welcome at the same address. There is no guaranteed timeline
for adding new features.

If this page changes, the "Last updated" date above changes with it.
