Demo App is a demo entry on this site. This page shows how a small, offline
TurkWave app of this kind lists the open-source components it ships.

## Bundled components

Demo App is built on its platform SDK and bundles no third-party open-source
libraries. It runs entirely on your device and makes no network requests of
its own.

## If this changes

If a future version bundles a third-party component, that component, its
copyright notice, and the full text of its license will be listed on this page,
as those licenses require.

## Contact

Questions about the components used in Demo App can be sent to the address
shown on the Demo App page.
